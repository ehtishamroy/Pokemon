A slim, floor-standing trading card vending tower built around a large touchscreen. Customers browse on screen, pay by card, mobile wallet or cash, and collect their pack from the pickup tray. The Charizard brick-and-flame wrap makes it a talking point in shops, arcades and events.
- Large touchscreen storefront
- Card, mobile wallet and cash payment options
- Multi-language screen
- Custom wrap available

## 2. THE TCG TOWER — PIKACHU EDITION
The same touchscreen tower in a bright Pikachu and lightning wrap, for locations that want a lighter, family-friendly look. Same screen, payments and pickup tray as the Charizard edition.
- Same features as the Charizard edition
- Pikachu and lightning artwork
- Custom wrap available

## 3. THE TCG SHOWCASE
A wide, two-door machine with a big front touchscreen that shows your cards like a digital shelf. The second door holds the payment section with the card reader. The blue Pikachu and Poké Ball wrap catches attention from across the room.
- Large touchscreen storefront
- Separate payment door with card reader
- PUSH pickup flap
- Custom wrap available

## 4. THE TCG CLASSIC
A full-size, glass-front machine in Poké Ball red and white. Customers see the actual packs on the shelves, choose on the side touchscreen, pay, and collect from the PUSH flap. It's the classic vending look for busy spots.
- Glass front showing the real products
- Side touchscreen
- Card reader
- Custom wrap available

## 5. THE TCG PRO
Our premium glass-front machine, with a stadium wrap and a glowing cyan LED frame. It displays the packs behind a large window, with a side touchscreen and payment on the second door.
- Large glass display window
- Glowing LED frame
- Side touchscreen and card reader
- Custom wrap available
